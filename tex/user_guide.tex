\section{Player Controls}

Player movement is controlled through the \texttt{WASD} keys.
\begin{itemize}
\item\texttt{w} - up
\item\texttt{a} - left
\item\texttt{s} - down 
\item\texttt{d} - right 
\end{itemize}

The player can roll, giving them a small period invulnerability whilst rolling.
\begin{itemize}
\item\texttt{<space>} - roll
\end{itemize}

The UI at the top left gives an indication of when rolling is available, as there is a small cooldown in which the player cannot roll again.

The player is given a wooden sword for attacking. A visual indicator will display the hitbox of the weapon's attack. Enemies within this hitbox will be damaged.
\begin{itemize}
\item\texttt{k} - attack
\end{itemize}

\section{Enemy AI}

Enemies damage the player when they hit the player. The player's hit-box is the bottom half, to give the 2.5D illusion. Enemies can be hit by a weapon, and are given 2 health-points. Each piece of damage takes 1 hit-point.

Enemies have three states. When far away from the player, the hunt: following a path towards the player. When within a certain distance, they will jump: a faster movement pointed directly at the player. When enemies are damaged, they are hurt: their appearance will fade, and they will not be able to move. They are still vulnerable to further attack in this state, and can still damage the player.

\section{Rounds}

The game is split into increasingly difficult rounds, which end when all enemies are spawned and killed.
